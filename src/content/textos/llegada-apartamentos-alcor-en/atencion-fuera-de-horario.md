## After-hours support

Alcor kept an assisted option for last-minute changes and document issues. The person helping opens the booking, identifies the outstanding step and explains how to continue. If the query passes to another shift, the remaining action is recorded beside the arrival status.

The new process also allows staff to update instructions when hours change. They review the message guests receive and the steps guests take after opening it, paying particular attention to the steps they need to complete on a phone.
