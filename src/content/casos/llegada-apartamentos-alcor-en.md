## Context and challenge

Guests arrive at Apartamentos Alcor throughout the day. For arrivals outside front desk hours, they often had to look through different messages for the required documents, access instructions and the support number. Staff first had to confirm which steps the guest had completed before they could help.

The difficulty was most apparent when the expected arrival time changed. Guests might have prepared their documents but still be unsure whether they needed to do anything else before entering. Answering the question could mean piecing together a conversation that had started on another channel.

## Solution

Alcor brought pre-arrival instructions and checks into one process. Before travelling, guests can see which details they need to prepare and how to ask for help. The team checks the booking status to see which arrivals can continue and which still need review.

The process keeps entry instructions alongside the information guests need on arrival. The confirmation message therefore remains a reference point even when arrival is delayed.
