## The challenge at arrival

Cetina Hotels wanted a common process for its boutique properties that still left room for personal service. Before the change, front desk staff collected and checked documents and transferred information manually. The workload was especially noticeable when several guests arrived together.

## Connecting registration to the PMS

Cetina Hotels began piloting Binding Identity in 2022. It reads and verifies documents, prepares the registration and supports electronic signatures. The integration transfers the data to SIHOT.PMS, where it appears in the guest record. Once the data is verified, Fast Check-in reduces the number of steps on subsequent visits.

The figures reflect the experience described in the [joint Cetina Hotels, SIHOT and Binding Identity case study](https://sihot.com/es/casos-de-exito/cetina-hotels-y-sihot-binding-identity/). The English quotation and case text are translations for editorial review.
