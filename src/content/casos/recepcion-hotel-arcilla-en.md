## Context and challenge

At Hotel Arcilla, arrivals peak in the late afternoon. Some bookings have all the information they need, while others require an extra check before the key can be handed over.

Initially, each shift used different screens to confirm the document, the signature and the booking. If a step was still outstanding, the person taking over had to piece together what had already happened.

### What the front desk needed to solve

- Provide a single place to check arrival status.
- Reduce duplicate manual entries.
- Keep an assisted process for exceptions.

## Solution

The team adopted a shared workflow that scanned documents, captured electronic signatures and updated bookings. Staff could continue welcoming guests while the system showed which steps remained.

### Rolling out the process by shift

The team reviewed representative arrivals before expanding the workflow. They focused on handovers, bookings for multiple people and documents requiring a manual check.

They documented when to return to the assisted process. The supporting documentation used clear language and action names that staff could recognise on the screens.

### Handling exceptions

Arrivals with incomplete information remained visible in the same booking. Staff could identify the outstanding step and continue without asking guests to repeat checks they had already completed.
