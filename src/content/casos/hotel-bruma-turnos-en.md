## Context and challenge

At Hotel Bruma, some arrivals were still in progress when shifts changed, and incoming staff had to consult notes kept in several places.

## Solution

The front desk added the status of the document scan, the signature and the next action to the booking. At handover, the incoming team could pick up from that point and request just the outstanding check.
